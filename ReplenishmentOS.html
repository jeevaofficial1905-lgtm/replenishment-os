Replenishment formula
Sales rate = Average of top 2 months in selected period (per channel, per SKU)
Adj. EBO = Rate × (Total stores ÷ Current stores) × (1 + EBO growth%)
Cover months = Cover days ÷ 30
Replenishment need = (Adjusted rate × Cover months) − Current stock
